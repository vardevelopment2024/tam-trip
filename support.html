Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a trip?

Tap the "+" button on the main screen and follow the instructions to create a new trip.

How do I add places to a trip?

Search for places or select them on the map and add them to your trip.

How do I delete a trip?

Swipe left on a trip in the list or open it and use the delete option.

How do I sign in?

You can sign in using your Google or Apple account from the settings screen.
