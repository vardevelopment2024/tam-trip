Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I create a trip? — Tap the "+" button on the main screen and follow the instructions.
  • How do I add places to a trip? — Search for places or select them on the map and add them to your trip.
  • How do I delete a trip? — Swipe left on a trip in the list or open it and use the delete option.
